# Co-Invest Earn and vault research

Use only read-only Earn or vault tools exposed by the connected Co-Invest service. Do not assume these tools exist: if none are available, explain that the requested data cannot be retrieved through this connection. Do not switch endpoints or call raw APIs to bypass that limitation.

Compare only terms returned by the tools, such as yield basis and period, fees, liquidity, lockups, and stated risks. Distinguish historical or estimated yields from guarantees, and do not extrapolate missing values. This skill is for research only: never initiate deposits, withdrawals, transfers, or other fund movements.
